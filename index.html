FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it completely free?
Yes! Paisa Tracker is built specifically for students and is 100% free with no premium paywalls for basic budgeting, goal setting, and tracking.
Is my financial data secure?
Absolutely. We do not link directly to your real bank accounts or store private credentials. All mock transactions are stored locally on your device (browser LocalStorage) ensuring bank-grade absolute privacy.
Does it support Indian Currency (₹)?
Yes, Paisa Tracker defaults to Indian Rupees (₹), and you can toggle to US Dollars ($) or other currencies inside the Settings panel instantly.
What is the AI Coach?
The AI Coach is a conversational chatbot helper that reads your mock transaction logs to flag warning trends, predict bank balances, and suggest sub optimizations.
